Short Description (Hero Section)
Create customizable testimonial sections in the WordPress block editor to display customer reviews, star ratings, or video feedback with responsive layouts and 40+ ready-made designs.


Thumbnail Image (Hero Section): .screenshots/landing-features/hero-icon.png — 1200x1200, transparent

Why Choose Testimonials?
Customer reviews should look convincing and belong to your design. Traditional testimonial blocks often lack the layout range, media support, and trust signals needed for modern, conversion-focused websites.

The Problem with Traditional Testimonial Sections
* One or two rigid layouts, so every site built with them looks the same
* Text-only quotes, with no video, audio or rating badges
* Reviews retyped page by page instead of reused
* Columns and gaps that need custom CSS to hold up on mobile

A Smarter Way to Show Social Proof
Testimonials makes it simple and convincing:
   * Add reviews using a Gutenberg block, with 40+ layouts picked visually
   * Write each review once in the Testimonials post type and reuse it site-wide
   * Official style score badges for Google, Trustpilot, G2, Facebook and Capterra
   * Video and audio reviews with popup lightbox playback
   * Collect reviews from visitors and approve each one before it goes live
   * Fully responsive and mobile-ready

1. 40+ Layouts, Picked Visually
Choose from grids, gradient-border grids, sliders, 3D carousels, masonry columns, avatar lists, card stacks, speech bubbles, timelines, quote boxes, popup modals and marquee tickers. Every layout is picked from the block placeholder or the Testimonial Block Switcher, with no CSS to write.
2. Official-Style Rating & Trust Badges
Display review score badges styled after Google Business, Trustpilot, G2, Facebook Recommendations and Capterra, plus Verified Buyer seals and trust badges. You set the score and review count yourself as block attributes, so nothing is fetched and nothing phones home.
3. Video & Audio Testimonials
Share video reviews from YouTube, Vimeo or your own Media Library with popup lightbox playback, or publish voice note reviews with waveform styling. A self-hosted file makes no external request at all, and an embed loads only after a visitor clicks play.
4. Collect Reviews & Manage Submissions
Drop the Testimonial Form block on any page and let customers send in their own reviews. Every submission arrives as pending under Testimonials > Submissions for you to approve, reject or delete, then flows straight into any layout on the site.


All Features (with title & short description)
40+ Block Layouts
Grids, sliders, carousels, masonry, avatar lists, card stacks, speech bubbles, timelines, quote boxes, popup modals and marquee tickers, all switched visually from the block itself.


Review Score Badges
Official style Google, Trustpilot, G2, Facebook and Capterra badges. The score and review count are block attributes you set yourself, not live data.


Verified Buyer & Trust Badges
Verified Buyer seals, guarantee and award badges, and a client logo wall as a grid or carousel.


Video Testimonials
YouTube, Vimeo or self-hosted MP4 reviews with popup lightbox playback. The embed loads only after a visitor clicks play.


Audio Testimonials
Voice note style reviews with waveform styling, for customers who will talk but not appear on camera.


Testimonial Collection Form
A front-end review form for any page, with name, designation, rating and review fields you can switch on or off.


Submissions Approval Dashboard
Every submission lands as pending under Testimonials > Submissions. Approve, reject or delete before anything reaches your visitors.


Testimonials Custom Post Type
Write each review once under Testimonials > Add New, then set Content Source to Testimonials CPT in any block to reuse it site-wide.


Rating Summary & Stats
Overall score, star distribution bars, satisfaction counters, comparison tables and FAQ-style review accordions.


Excerpt & Read More Control
Trim long reviews to a set length with an expandable Read More / Show Less button so cards stay evenly sized.


Responsive Device Controls
Set columns, column gap and row gap independently for desktop, tablet and mobile. No custom CSS needed.


Full Typography & Colour Controls
Font size, weight, transform, line height and colour for reviewer name, designation, review text and star icons, plus card background, padding, border and shadow.


Performance Optimized
One shared front-end bundle across all forty blocks rather than forty copies, so a page carrying three of them fetches one file.


No Tracking, No Telemetry
Collects no analytics, telemetry or usage data, and does not phone home on activation.

Plugin FAQs (Feature Related)
Q: Can I use multiple testimonial sections on one site?
 Yes, you can add unlimited Testimonials blocks on different pages or posts.


Q: Are the testimonial layouts responsive?
 Yes, all 40+ layouts are fully responsive, with separate column and gap controls for desktop, tablet and mobile.


Q: Can I collect reviews from my visitors?
 Yes, the Testimonial Form block collects reviews from any page, and every submission waits for your approval before it goes live.


Q: Are the Google, Trustpilot and G2 badges pulled live from those platforms?
 No, each badge is a static widget. You enter the score and review count yourself, so keep them up to date as your real rating changes.


Q: Can I reuse the same reviews across multiple pages?
 Yes, create them once under Testimonials > Add New and set Content Source to Testimonials CPT in any block.


Q: Does switching layouts keep my content?
 No, picking a different layout replaces the block with that layout's own starting content. Pick the layout first, then add your real reviews.


Q: Is everything free?
 Yes, all 40+ layouts, badges, video and audio reviews, the collection form and the submissions dashboard are in the free version. There is no Pro tier and no paywalled feature.
