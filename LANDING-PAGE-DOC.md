## Core Information

**Plugin Name:** Testimonials – Customer Reviews, Ratings, Badges & Video Testimonials

**WP.org URL:** https://wordpress.org/plugins/b-testimonials-block/

**Short Description:** Create customizable testimonial sections in the WordPress block editor to display customer reviews, star ratings, or video feedback with responsive layouts and 40+ ready-made designs.

## Problem Statement

Traditional testimonial sections suffer from one or two rigid layouts, text-only quotes with no video or rating badges, reviews retyped page by page, and columns that need custom CSS to hold up on mobile.

## Key Solution Benefits

- Add testimonials via a Gutenberg block, with 40+ layouts picked visually
- Manage every review once in the Testimonials post type and reuse it site-wide
- Show official style score badges for Google, Trustpilot, G2, Facebook and Capterra
- Publish video and audio reviews with popup lightbox playback
- Collect reviews from visitors and approve each one before it goes live
- Fully responsive across devices

## Four Main Features

1. **40+ Layouts, Picked Visually** – Choose from grids, sliders, carousels, masonry columns, speech bubbles, timelines, card stacks and marquee tickers, all switched from the block placeholder with no CSS
2. **Official-Style Rating & Trust Badges** – Display Google, Trustpilot, G2, Facebook and Capterra score badges plus Verified Buyer seals, with the score and review count set as block attributes
3. **Video & Audio Testimonials** – Share YouTube, Vimeo or self-hosted video reviews with lightbox playback, and voice note reviews with waveform styling
4. **Collect Reviews & Manage Submissions** – Front-end form on any page, with every submission held as pending for you to approve, reject or delete

## FAQs

- Supports unlimited testimonials across the site
- Fully responsive and works with any theme, including the Site Editor
- Reviews can be collected from visitors and approved before publishing
- Score badges are static widgets — you enter the score and review count yourself
- Everything is free; there is no paywalled feature and no Pro upsell
- Switching layouts replaces the block's content, so pick the layout first
