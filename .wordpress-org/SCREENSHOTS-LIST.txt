1. Activate Testimonials from the Plugins screen.
2. Go to Pages -> Add New to start a new page.
3. Search "Testimonials" in the block inserter and add the block.
4. Pick one of the 40+ layouts from the placeholder.
5. Type your reviews and open the sidebar to customise.
6. Open the Testimonial Block Switcher to browse every layout.
7. Switching layouts replaces the block with that layout's own default content.
8. Keep editing your content, then Publish.
